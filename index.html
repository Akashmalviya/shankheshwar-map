Drawing

Revised layout plan of residential colony for Shri Rishabh Grah Nirman Sahakari Society, Indore. Approved by Town & Country Planning, Indore (2006–07). Dimensions in metres.

This view is redrawn from the scan. Block positions follow the drawing; plot sizes are the figures written on it. Strip plots along the edges taper, so their depths here are interpolated between the marked values.

In 3D, the houses are illustrative (ground to G+2 with typical setbacks). The plan only fixes plot boundaries, not buildings.
